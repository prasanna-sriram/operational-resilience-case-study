[Project overview](/README.md)

**Primary-source checks carried forward from 1 November 2026. Public-project structure prepared 2 November 2026.** This structural edition does not claim new source checks. Recheck relevant requirements before applying the analysis or publishing a later case.

## Scope and status

The E-21 implementation letter distinguishes the September 2026 adherence/testing-start milestone from completion of testing for all critical operations by September 2027. Operation tolerances, individual recovery targets and observed test results remain separate claims. The PSP reference is relevant only where that distinct entity/activity scope is introduced.

## Access limits

Public publisher descriptions support high-level ISO and AICPA distinctions. Full licensed standards/guide texts were not available for a fresh clause-by-clause conformity review. Templates are original teaching tools and do not reproduce complete requirements. For Quebec topics, CAI guidance was available; direct consolidated-law retrieval previously failed and detailed legal conclusions need the authoritative legislation.
