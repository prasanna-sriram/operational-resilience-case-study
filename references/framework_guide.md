# Framework and Evidence Guide

[Project overview](/README.md) · [Source register](/references/source_register.md)

## Three types of statements

| Type | What it establishes | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| Primary-reference proposition | A bounded external requirement, expectation or concept | That the fictional company has met it |
| Scenario fact | A premise supplied for this case | A verified real-world event or complete population |
| Analysis or proposed method | A reasoned way to investigate or treat the stated exposure | A regulator-prescribed template or automatic compliance conclusion |

## Applying references

Establish entity and activity scope, identify the issuer and reference type, check edition and effective status, then identify the evidence relevant to the decision. A topic relationship is not a claim of equivalence between regimes.

The E-21 implementation letter distinguishes the September 2026 adherence/testing-start milestone from completion of testing for all critical operations by September 2027. Operation tolerances, individual recovery targets and observed test results remain separate claims. The PSP reference is relevant only where that distinct entity/activity scope is introduced.

## Using the artefacts

The worked records contain invented examples. Blank templates require an explicit scenario, source references, evidence scope, unknowns and decision owner. Do not turn a filled cell into a verified fact without evidence.

## Drawing a conclusion

State what was examined, what was observed, what remains untested, and what action or decision follows. A passing sample does not prove universal coverage. A documented acceptance does not remove the exposure it accepts. Record material changes in the [change log](/CHANGELOG.md).
