### Worked operation boundary

| Field | Fictional entry |
|---|---|
| Candidate operation | Receive and triage urgent claims |
| Start | Customer attempts contact through an approved channel |
| End | Claim recorded and urgency assigned with an accountable next owner |
| Customer outcome | Access to timely assessment and next-step guidance |
| Excluded from this initial boundary | Final adjudication and settlement; assess dependencies and interfaces separately |
| Disruption concern | Inability to identify urgent cases and route them to available staff |
| Classification status | Proposed; rationale and authority require confirmation |

The exclusion is an analytical boundary, not permission to ignore downstream harm. The assessment must explain how failure at the interface affects the wider operation.
