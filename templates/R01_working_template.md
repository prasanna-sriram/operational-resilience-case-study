# R01 - Working Template

[Case chapter](/chapters/R01_start_with_the_operation_not_the_server.md) · [Worked example](/artefacts/R01_worked_analysis.md)

**Illustrative original template.** This is not a regulator-issued form or complete conformity checklist. Fill it for a specified scenario, retaining unknowns and supporting evidence.

## Design considerations

### Reusable template

Record the delivered service; affected users; start/end; products and channels; necessary steps; harm mechanisms; criticality rationale; owner; approver; dependencies; exclusions; review triggers.

Ask two people to use the boundary independently. If they identify different populations and outcomes, refine the definition before comparing test results. Consistent language is part of making evidence comparable.
